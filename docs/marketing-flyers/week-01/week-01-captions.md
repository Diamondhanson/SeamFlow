## Wednesday · The notebook got lost (tailors)

**🇬🇧**
Tailors, be honest: how many measurement notebooks have you lost? 📓

One torn page, one coffee stain, and a client's sizes are gone. Then comes the awkward call: "Can you come back so I can measure you again?"

With SeamFlow, you save each client's measurements once. Already have them on paper? Take a photo of the sheet and SeamFlow reads the numbers for you. You check every value, save, and they're ready for the next order.

🧵 Coming soon to Cameroon. Save this post, and tag a tailor who still works from a notebook.
